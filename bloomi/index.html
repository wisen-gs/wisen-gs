Bloomi!

Bloomi! is a cosy flower-shop puzzle game for iPhone. Sort the flower bunches, tie matching bouquets and send them off before the clock runs out.

Support

For help or feedback, email us at hello@wisen.gs. Please include your nickname, which you can find in the game under Settings, and your iPhone model, so we can help faster.

Purchases

A purchase did not arrive. Make sure you are online, then close and reopen the game. Unfinished purchases are completed when the game starts. If your coins are still missing, email us with the date of the purchase.

Refunds. Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Coins and progress are saved with an anonymous player ID on your device. If you delete the app, your coins and progress may not come back.

Delete my data

In the game, open Settings › Delete my data and confirm. You need an internet connection: the game first deletes your data from our servers and only then resets the game on your device. If our servers cannot be reached, nothing is deleted and you can try again later. If you cannot use the app, email hello@wisen.gs with your nickname.
