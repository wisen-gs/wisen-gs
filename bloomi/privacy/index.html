Bloomi Privacy

Effective 26 October 2026

This notice explains what information the Bloomi game for iOS collects, why, and what you can do about it. Bloomi is currently in early testing. We keep data collection to what the game needs to work.

Who we are

Bloomi is made by Wisen Ltd, the controller of the personal information described here. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 10564914.

For privacy enquiries, contact hello@wisen.gs.

What we collect

Bloomi does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. No account or sign-up is needed. The game creates a random player ID on your device. It is not linked to your Apple ID, your name or your email address.

We store your game progress, scores and an auto-generated nickname with that player ID:

Nickname. A name the game generates at random, for example “JellyPanda#482”. You do not choose it and it is not your real name.

Game progress. Your current level, the highest level you have completed, the number of games you have played, and your daily streaks.

Game results. For each completed level: the level number, your score, the number of moves, the time taken, the sequence of moves you made, and when you finished. We keep the move sequence so we can check that scores are genuine.

Timestamps. When your player record was created and when you last opened the game.

When the game connects to our servers, some basic technical information is also processed so that the service can work, such as your device’s IP address, an app installation identifier and limited diagnostic information.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the game (saving your progress, loading levels and keeping your results), to show leaderboards, to adjust game settings without an app update, and to prevent cheating and abuse and keep the service working.

Our lawful basis is that this processing is necessary to provide the game you have chosen to play, together with our legitimate interest in keeping it fair, secure and working.

What other players can see

Your nickname and scores may appear on leaderboards that other players can see. Other players do not see your real identity, because the game does not have it.

Other players’ copies of the game may also be able to access the rest of your player record: your nickname, your progress and streaks, and when you started and last played. The game does not display this extra information, and your player record does not contain your real name or any contact details.

Advertising & tracking

Bloomi currently contains no advertising and no third-party analytics. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not sell your information. We do not use the Apple advertising identifier.

If this changes, for example if we add ads or in-app purchases, we will update this notice before releasing that version, and ask for your permission where the law or Apple requires it.

Service providers

We use Google Cloud services to store game data. Data may be stored and processed on servers outside your country.

If you test Bloomi through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple shares certain information with us, such as crash reports and the feedback you choose to send. Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice explains this.

How long we keep it

We keep your player record and game results while the game is running, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your choices & rights

Delete your data. Email hello@wisen.gs and include your nickname, which you can find in the game under Settings. We will delete your player record and results.

Stop collection. Delete the app. Your device then stops sending data. Data stored before that stays on our servers until you ask us to delete it.

Depending on where you live, for example in the UK or the EU, you can also ask to access or correct your personal information, restrict its use, or receive a portable copy. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. To make any request, email hello@wisen.gs.

You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or with your local data protection authority.

Children

Bloomi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used Bloomi, contact us and we will delete the related player record.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will update the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will also tell players in the game or in the release notes.
