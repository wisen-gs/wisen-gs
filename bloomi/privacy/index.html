Bloomi! Privacy

Effective 16 November 2026

This notice explains what information the Bloomi! game for iPhone collects, why, and what you can do about it. We keep data collection to what the game needs to work, to understand how the game is played, and to deliver purchases.

Who we are

Bloomi! is made by Wisen Ltd, the controller of the personal information described here. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 10564914.

For privacy enquiries, contact hello@wisen.gs.

In short

No account or sign-up is needed. The game creates a random player ID. It does not know your name, email address or Apple ID.

We store your game progress and results so the game works and leaderboards are fair.

We use Google Analytics to understand how the game is played, for example which levels are too hard. Analytics is not linked to your player ID, and you can switch it off in Settings.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

No advertising, no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites, and no use of the Apple advertising identifier.

You can delete your data at any time in the game under Settings › Delete my data.

What we collect

Player ID and nickname. When you first open the game, it creates a random player ID and a nickname generated at random, for example “SunnyTulip#482”. You do not choose the nickname and it is not your real name. The player ID is not linked to your Apple ID, your name or your email address.

Game progress. Your current level, the highest level you have completed, the number of games you have played, your daily streaks, when your player record was created and when you last opened the game.

Game results. For each completed level: the level number, your score, the number of moves, the time taken, the sequence of moves you made, and when you finished. We keep the move sequence so we can check that scores are genuine.

Gameplay analytics. If analytics is switched on, the game records events such as starting, winning or losing a level, using a booster, seeing an in-game offer, making a purchase and finishing the tutorial. Google Analytics also processes technical information such as the app version, device model, operating system version, language, an app-instance identifier and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address. Analytics data is not linked to your player ID or nickname and never includes your name or contact details.

Purchases. If you buy coins or the Starter Pack, Apple processes the payment. The game receives a confirmation of the transaction so it can deliver what you bought; this is checked and kept on your device. Coins, boosters and the record that you have bought the Starter Pack are kept on your device, not on our servers. We never receive your name, card details or Apple ID.

Game Center (optional). If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the game submits your star total and highest level to Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy, and your Game Center name and entries are visible to other players according to your Game Center settings. We do not store your Game Center name on our servers. You never need Game Center to play.

Technical information. When the game connects to our servers, some basic technical information is processed so that the service can work, such as your device’s IP address, an app installation identifier and limited diagnostic information.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or the Apple advertising identifier.

How we use it

We use this information to run the game (saving your progress, loading levels and keeping your results), to deliver purchases, to show leaderboards, to adjust game settings without an app update, to understand how the game is played so we can improve its levels, difficulty and in-game offers, and to prevent cheating and abuse and keep the service working.

Our lawful basis for running the game, saving your progress and delivering purchases is that this is necessary to provide the game you have chosen to play. For fair play, security and analytics, we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working, fair and enjoyable. You can switch analytics off at any time in Settings.

What other players can see

Your nickname and scores may appear on leaderboards that other players can see. Other players do not see your real identity, because the game does not have it.

Other players’ copies of the game may also be able to access the rest of your player record: your nickname, your progress and streaks, and when you started and last played. The game does not display this extra information, and your player record does not contain your real name or any contact details.

Advertising & tracking

Bloomi! contains no advertising. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, we do not sell your information or share it for advertising, and we do not use the Apple advertising identifier. Advertising features in our analytics are switched off, and our analytics is not linked to any advertising account.

If this ever changes, we will update this notice before releasing that version, and ask for your permission where the law or Apple requires it.

Service providers

Google stores game data, runs our analytics and delivers game settings for us. Google processes this data on our behalf.

Apple handles in-app purchase payments, Game Center and app distribution, under Apple’s own privacy policy.

Data may be stored and processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where UK or EU data is transferred, it is protected by the safeguards our providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Player record and game results: while the game is running, or until you delete them.

Analytics: kept by Google for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.

Purchases: we do not keep our own copy of your payment records; Apple keeps them. Purchase confirmations on your device are removed when you delete your data or the app.

Your choices & rights

Delete your data. In the game, open Settings › Delete my data and confirm. The game first deletes your player ID, your player record and your game results from our servers, so you need an internet connection. If our servers cannot be reached, nothing is deleted and the game tells you, so you can try again later. Once the server step succeeds, the game also clears its data on your device, including coins, boosters and the analytics identifier, and starts again as a new install. This cannot be undone. If you cannot use the app, email hello@wisen.gs and include your nickname, which you can find in the game under Settings.

Analytics. You can switch analytics off at any time in Settings › Analytics. The game then stops sending analytics events.

Stop all collection. Delete the app. Your device then stops sending data. Data stored before that stays on our servers until you delete it as described above.

Analytics data is not linked to your player ID, so we cannot find it by nickname; it is deleted automatically as described above. Game Center leaderboard entries are held by Apple and are managed through your Game Center settings.

Depending on where you live, for example in the UK or the EU, you can also ask to access or correct your personal information, restrict its use, or receive a portable copy. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. To make any request, email hello@wisen.gs.

You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or with your local data protection authority.

Children

Bloomi! is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used Bloomi!, contact us and we will delete the related player record.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will update the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will also tell players in the game or in the release notes.
