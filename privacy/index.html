How Wisen Ltd handles information from this website and company enquiries.

Who we are

Wisen Ltd is the controller of personal information we receive through company enquiries. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 10564914.

For privacy enquiries, contact hello@wisen.gs.

Information we use

If you email us, we receive your email address, any name you provide and the contents of your message. Please only send information needed for your enquiry.

We use this information to respond and manage the conversation. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in handling company enquiries. Where you ask us to take steps towards a contract, processing necessary for those steps may instead be based on that request.

Contacting us is voluntary. Without sufficient contact information, we may be unable to reply.

Cookies & hosting

The website code does not add advertising cookies, analytics trackers, contact forms or newsletter subscriptions. Fonts and artwork are served with the site rather than loaded from third-party font or image services.

Hosting and security providers may process technical data, such as IP addresses, browser details, request times and requested pages, to deliver the site and prevent abuse. The hosting platform may also use cookies required for access and security. These are separate from advertising or visitor analytics.

Sharing & retention

Website delivery and email correspondence involve hosting, security and email service providers. Information may also be disclosed where required by law. We do not use enquiry details for advertising or sell them.

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to answer the enquiry and manage any resulting relationship, taking account of legal record-keeping needs and any unresolved disputes. Technical records are subject to the hosting provider’s retention practices.

Provider processing locations and any international transfer arrangements depend on the services used. Contact us for details relevant to your enquiry.

Your choices & rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, restrict its use, or receive a portable copy.

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. To make a request, email hello@wisen.gs. We may need information to verify your identity.

You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Games & policy updates

This notice covers the company website and company enquiries. It does not describe the data practices of any game or app. Product-specific privacy information will be provided alongside the relevant product.

We may update this notice as the website or our practices change. The date at the top shows when this version was last revised.
